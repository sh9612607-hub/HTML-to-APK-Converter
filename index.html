🔐 Android Keystore & Google Play Release Helper

To publish your APK or AAB on Google Play Store, you need a signed release key. Run this standard command in your terminal:

keytool -genkey -v -keystore release-key.jks -alias my-app-key -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000

Remember your keystore password and alias. You will use them in Android Studio under Build > Generate Signed Bundle / APK.
